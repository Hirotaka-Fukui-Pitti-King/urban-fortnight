# Stage 1: Ising Expectations and Social Interactions

**Status:** Planned. **Dependencies:** Stage 0.

## Central research question

Do peer interactions produce belief coordination and new nonlinear policy responses beyond ordinary heterogeneous expectations?

## Economic mechanisms

- Economically defined binary expectation states
- Network or mean-field interaction term
- Probabilistic belief transitions and macro feedback
- Household decisions conditional on beliefs

## Economic closure and model caveats

- Specify precisely what is forecast and how information and beliefs enter choices.
- An Ising fixed point is not automatically a rational-expectations general equilibrium.
- Restore the Stage 0 rational-expectations rule in a matched no-social-interaction benchmark.

## Nested comparators

- rational-expectations baseline
- heterogeneous independent beliefs
- interacting beliefs

## Acceptance tests

- Finite-agent versus mean-field belief moments
- Fixed-point stability and multiplicity under stated parameter normalization
- Household feasibility and general equilibrium residuals
- Nested benchmark recovery

## Deliverables

- Belief dynamics with economic interpretation
- Phase diagrams and policy responses

**Progression gate:** The numerical equilibrium, accounting identities, convergence checks and nested comparator must be verified before moving to the next stage. Record the code revision, seeds and tolerances in `results/`.
